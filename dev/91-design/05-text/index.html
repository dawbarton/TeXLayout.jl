Text and documents

The math pipeline sets one formula. A second layer handles text with inline and display math, over several lines. Its entry point is layout_document, which returns a measured TeXBox:

struct TeXBox
    boxes::Vector{LayoutBox}   # every line, positioned
    width::Float64             # em
    ascent::Float64            # em above the first baseline
    descent::Float64           # em below the last baseline
end

Document AST (src/document.jl)

parse_document(input) reads text by default and enters math with $…$, \(…\), $$…$$, \[…\], or a top-level display environment. It returns a Document, a Vector{Block}:

TypeRole
TextAttrsthe resolved style of a span: text family, weight and shape slot, size, and TextFeatures
TextFeaturesbackend-independent features; currently small_caps
TextSpana run of characters with one TextAttrs
TextRun, MathRuna stretch of a line: shaped text, or a math Node with its style
Linethe runs between \\ breaks
ParagraphBlock, DisplayBlock, ParagraphBreakBlocka paragraph of lines, a display, or a blank-line break

Text commands change independent axes of TextAttrs: family (\textrm, \textsf, \texttt), weight (\textbf), shape (\textit), and features (\textsc). \emph toggles italic relative to its surroundings, and \textnormal is the only full reset. \text and \mbox open a group that inherits the current style. The same transitions, in src/text_styles.jl, serve \text{…} inside math. align, aligned, gather, gathered, split, and equation at the top level become DisplayBlocks.

Whitespace

Document text follows LaTeX. A single newline is one space, a continuation line's indentation collapses, a blank line starts a paragraph, and leading and trailing whitespace at line and block boundaries is dropped. _DocBuilder implements this with a deferred space (pending_space, at_line_start). Spaces inside {…} and around inline math are significant. % comments are handled in the lexer (see Parsing).

~ is a significant non-breaking space (pending_nbsp): one interword space, merged with adjacent ordinary whitespace, and, unlike ordinary whitespace, never dropped at a line or paragraph boundary. There is no soft line breaking yet, so its non-breaking property cannot be observed.

Shaping (src/shaping.jl)

A TextShaper turns a TextSpan into positioned glyphs: shape_span(shaper, span, family, scale) returns a TeXBox in em, baseline at y = 0 and first glyph at x = 0, whose width is the total advance. Every shaper emits GlyphID elements naming the exact font file that the text-font fallback chose, and must apply the span's TextFeatures faithfully or reject them.

  • MetricShaper (the default) places one glyph per character with advances from hmtx, without substitution or kerning. It throws on a span with features rather than fake small capitals from scaled capitals. It must stay testable without HarfBuzz.
  • HarfBuzzShaper (ext/HarfBuzzExt.jl, loaded with HarfBuzzjll) shapes with HarfBuzz and maps `smallcapsto the OpenTypesmcp` feature. It does not reorder bidirectional paragraphs, expose language, script, or feature settings, or shape math glyphs and operators.

Document text always goes through LayoutOptions.shaper. Math \text{…} and \mbox{…} use the shaper only when one other than MetricShaper is passed to layout or generate_tex_elements, or when a text style needs it; otherwise they keep the upright-glyph path.

Composition (src/boxes.jl, src/compose.jl)

Composition measures with a small internal box tree, ShapedBox (laid-out boxes with their extents), HBox, and VBox, and flattens it with shape(box) into the Vector{LayoutBox} renderers consume. On top of it:

  • hlayout_math and hlayout_run lay out one math node or one run as a TeXBox. Inline math takes the larger of the layout's advance and its measured width, so space after a final script counts.
  • hconcat joins runs on a shared baseline.
  • vstack stacks lines and displays with line_height, lineskip, and the display and paragraph skips.
  • LayoutOptions holds those settings (align, width, line_height, lineskip, display_align, abovedisplayskip, belowdisplayskip, parskip, shaper); layout_document builds one from keywords over the session default (default_layout_options).

The internal box tree is the first step towards a box-and-glue representation for all layout; math layout still emits flat ranges. Line breaking, when it comes, is the natural point to finish that migration.
